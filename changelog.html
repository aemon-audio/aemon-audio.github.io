Changelog

Before the first public release

  • Aemon Suite installer: one setup for every plugin. It replaces the earlier test installers, keeps your presets, and never closes your DAW.
  • Aemon Limiter: loudness targets, “Make it loud safely”, equal-loudness compare, true peak, dither, 15 presets.
  • Limiter engine: true peaks now hold the ceiling at every setting tested, with the same latency as before.
  • Compressor: in Simple, the highlighted style always matches the settings, also after presets, undo and A/B.
  • Utility, EQ and Compressor: presets say what they do, with a one-line description.

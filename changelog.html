Changelog

0.2.0

  • Five new plugins: De-esser, Multiband, Saturation, Imager and Clipper. Nine in all.
  • The installer finds the Aemon plugins you already have, marks them as updates, and adds the new ones. Anything you left out on purpose stays out.
  • Presets in the De-esser and the Multiband set themselves to your sound.
  • Our automatic build only finished its plugin host test for the first plugin. It now runs for every plugin and stops the release if one fails. The four plugins in 0.1.0 had passed it on our own computer.
  • EQ: clearer description for “808: audible on phone speakers”.

0.1.0

  • First public release: Utility, EQ, Compressor and Limiter.
  • One installer for every plugin, and a ZIP. It keeps your presets and never closes your DAW.
